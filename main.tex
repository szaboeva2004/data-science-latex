\section{Introduction}

At the start of the project, we learned about benchmarking, I created the project repository and set up the environment so everything would run smoothly. This included organizing files and installing the needed packages.

Together with my teammates, we chose the encoders, scalers, and other tools to test. I was in charge of finding 20 different datasets from the recommended sources. This took time, as I made sure they varied in size and type to give us useful results.

\section{Experiments}

During the experimentation phase, we quickly realized that not all preprocessing configurations were compatible with every dataset. Certain encoders or scalers failed on specific data due to missing values, categorical anomalies, or incompatible formats. I was in charge of searching for new datasets, as we couldn’t always tell if the errors were caused by the datasets themselves or by the preprocessing steps. I also helped diagnose issues and refine the pipeline to ensure everything ran smoothly.

\section{Visualizations and Testing}

One of my primary responsibilities in the project was designing and conducting the performance evaluations. I implemented a range of visualizations to illustrate how different combinations of encoders and scalers performed across the selected datasets. These visualizations included bar plots and heatmaps, which helped us identify patterns and anomalies in model behavior.

\begin{figure}[h]
    \centering

    \caption{An example of the performance plots I generated during testing.}
    \label{fig:plot}
\end{figure}

Figure~\ref{fig:plot} presents one of the many plots I created to support our analysis. I also interpreted the results of these plots and documented the key insights in a shared notebook, which later served as a basis for our decisions on the final preprocessing configurations.

\section{Consensus Rankings and Aggregation}

For the final phase of the project, we aimed to derive consensus rankings of the different preprocessing pipelines based on performance across datasets. I was responsible for implementing most of the ranking and aggregation logic. This included calculating individual performance scores, normalizing results, and applying various ranking strategies to compare configurations objectively.

Additionally, I prepared and delivered the presentation for this section, explaining our methodology and summarizing our key findings. I also ensured that the implementation was robust, well-documented, and reproducible.

\section{Conclusion}

Throughout the project, I contributed significantly to both the technical and organizational aspects. From setting up the repository and gathering datasets to implementing experiments, visualizations, and ranking logic, my work was central to the success of the benchmarking pipeline. The insights gained from this process provided a deeper understanding of the impact of preprocessing choices on machine learning performance.
